Nota ringkas Take care

Setiap hentian memberi murid satu kad fakta selepas lulus. Ini kad bagi unit ini:

  • stressStress is the worried feeling you have when there is too much to do. Exercise, sleep and talking to a friend can reduce stress.
  • bullyingBullying is hurting or frightening someone again and again. If you see bullying, tell a teacher or a parent. Do not stay silent.
  • tense reviewHabit: present simple (I walk to school). Now: present continuous (I'm walking). Past: past simple (I walked). Experience: present perfect (I've walked). Future plan: be going to. Prediction: will.
  • should / mustshould = advice (You should rest). must = strong obligation (You must wear a helmet). shouldn't = advice not to (You shouldn't eat too much). mustn't = forbidden (You mustn't smoke).
  • appointmentAn appointment is a fixed time to see someone, such as a doctor. You make an appointment by phone or online.
  • symptomA symptom is a sign that you are ill: fever, cough, headache. Tell the doctor your symptoms and how long you've had them.
  • listen for adviceListen for should, shouldn't, must, mustn't and for the reason after because. They tell you the advice and why.
  • tense cluesTime words tell you the tense: every day (habit), now (action in progress), yesterday (past), since/for (present perfect), tomorrow (future).
  • trustworthyA trustworthy person is someone you can trust, such as a parent, a teacher or a counsellor. You can tell them your problems.
  • balancedA balanced diet has all kinds of food in the right amounts: rice, protein, vegetables and fruit. A balanced life has time for study, sleep, play and family.
  • advice with becauseGive advice and a reason: You should sleep early because your body needs rest. Use because for the reason, so for the result.
  • check the tenseBefore you send a message, check the time word and the tense: yesterday + past, now + continuous, since/for + present perfect.

Contoh soalan

4 daripada 54 soalan dalam unit ini. Soalan lain, termasuk soalan berajah, soalan bos dan TP6, dimainkan dalam permainan.

  1. TP1

    Someone who does not eat meat or fish is a

    1. school bully
    2. vegetarian
    3. team supporter
    4. sports champion
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: B. vegetarian
    A vegetarian does not eat meat or fish.

  2. TP2

    You ____ go to the doctor if you feel ill.

    1. mustn't
    2. didn't
    3. aren't
    4. should
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: D. should
    Advice: should + base verb.

  3. TP2

    He ____ go to the hospital if his condition gets serious.

    1. mustn't
    2. must
    3. did
    4. are
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: B. must
    A serious condition needs strong obligation: must.

  4. TP2

    You ____ react to a bully's behaviour with violence.

    1. must
    2. should
    3. mustn't
    4. did
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: C. mustn't
    Mustn't shows that something is forbidden.

Contoh ulasan PBD

Selepas murid bermain, Jejak TP English mencadangkan TP dan ulasan seperti di bawah. Guru menyemak, kemudian menyalin ulasan ke dalam rekod PBD.

TP1: Murid recognises a few basic words on health, personal issues and giving advice with a lot of support. Suggestion: revise the word cards in Stop 1 with a friend and use pictures to remember the meanings.

TP2: Murid understands key vocabulary on health, personal issues and giving advice but still makes basic grammar errors. Suggestion: practise short sentences using the target grammar every day.

TP3: Murid reads short texts on health, personal issues and giving advice and finds specific details accurately. Murid achieves the expected target for this unit.

TP4: Murid understands spoken English on health, personal issues and giving advice and identifies main ideas and specific details well. Murid is working towards exceeding the target.

TP5: Murid reads longer texts on health, personal issues and giving advice, makes good inferences and guesses the meaning of unfamiliar words from context. Very good progress.

TP6: Murid uses English accurately and independently on health, personal issues and giving advice, corrects errors and builds well-organised sentences. Murid exceeds the target and is an exemplary model to others.

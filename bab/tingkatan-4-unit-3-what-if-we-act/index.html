Nota ringkas What If We Act?

Setiap hentian memberi murid satu kad fakta selepas lulus. Ini kad bagi unit ini:

  • sustainableSustainable means able to continue without harming the environment. Sustainable farming protects the soil and uses less water.
  • carbon footprintYour carbon footprint is the amount of carbon dioxide that your activities produce. Walking and cycling reduce your carbon footprint; flying increases it.
  • zero, first, secondZero: If + present, present (facts): If ice melts, sea levels rise. First: If + present, will + base verb (real future): If we plant trees, the air will be cleaner. Second: If + past, would + base verb (unreal): If we lived on Mars, we would need oxygen.
  • unlessUnless = if ... not. Unless we act now, the problem will get worse. = If we don't act now, the problem will get worse. Do not use unless with a negative verb.
  • emissionsEmissions are gases released into the air. Transport, factories and power stations produce emissions, including carbon dioxide.
  • per personPer person means for each individual. Per person figures let us compare countries fairly, even if their populations are different.
  • if-clausesListen to the verb tense in the if clause: present = real possibility (will in the result), past = imaginary (would in the result).
  • unlessWhen you hear unless, think of if not: Unless we act, things will get worse = If we don't act, things will get worse.
  • campaignA campaign is a planned series of actions to achieve an aim. An email can invite people to join a campaign.
  • persuasive languagePersuasive texts use conditionals (If we act now, we will ...), strong adjectives (urgent, serious) and questions (Will you join us?).
  • persuade with ifTo persuade, show the result of action and inaction: If we recycle, we will save resources. Unless we act, the problem will grow. Use strong words: urgent, serious, essential.
  • match the clausesCheck each if sentence: present + will (real), past + would (unreal), present + present (fact). Do not mix them.

Contoh soalan

5 daripada 54 soalan dalam unit ini. Soalan lain, termasuk soalan berajah, soalan bos dan TP6, dimainkan dalam permainan.

  1. TP1

    Which word means 'to make something smaller or less in amount'?

    1. increase
    2. expand
    3. produce
    4. reduce
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: D. reduce
    To reduce is to make something less.

  2. TP1

    Which of these is a fossil fuel?

    1. coal
    2. wind
    3. sunshine
    4. water power
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: A. coal
    Coal, oil and natural gas are fossil fuels. Wind, sunshine and water are renewable.

  3. TP1

    Gases such as carbon dioxide that trap heat in the atmosphere are called ____ gases.

    1. kitchen
    2. greenhouse
    3. garden
    4. playground
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: B. greenhouse
    Greenhouse gases trap heat and cause global warming.

  4. TP2

    If you heat ice, it ____ into water.

    1. will turn
    2. turned
    3. would turn
    4. turns
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: D. turns
    Zero conditional (a general fact): If + present simple, present simple.

  5. TP2

    If we recycle more, the landfill ____ smaller.

    1. will be
    2. would be
    3. is being
    4. were
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: A. will be
    First conditional (real possibility): If + present simple, will + base verb.

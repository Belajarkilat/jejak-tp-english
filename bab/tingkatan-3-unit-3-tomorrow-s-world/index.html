Nota ringkas Tomorrow's World

Setiap hentian memberi murid satu kad fakta selepas lulus. Ini kad bagi unit ini:

  • artificial intelligenceArtificial intelligence (AI) is computer technology that can do things that normally need human thinking, such as understanding speech or recognising faces.
  • privacyPrivacy is your right to keep your personal information secret. Online, we protect our privacy with strong passwords.
  • four ways to talk about the futurePresent simple: timetables (The train leaves at 6). Present continuous: arrangements (I'm meeting Zack at 5). Going to: plans and evidence (It's going to rain). Will/won't: predictions, promises, quick decisions (I'll help you).
  • might, may, will, probablywill = I'm sure (It will rain). probably will = I think so (It will probably rain). may / might = it's possible (It may rain). The meaning of might/may is weaker than will.
  • exhibitionAn exhibition (expo) is a big show where companies or people present their work. Visitors can see, try and sometimes buy things.
  • admissionAdmission is the money you pay to go into a place or an event. Admission is free means you do not have to pay.
  • certain or possible?Listen for words that show how sure the speaker is: definitely, certainly, probably, perhaps, might, may. They change the meaning of a prediction.
  • plan or prediction?A plan: I'm going to study medicine. An arrangement: I'm seeing the doctor at four. A prediction: Doctors will use more robots in the future.
  • automationAutomation means using machines or computers to do work that people did before. Automation can make work faster, but some jobs may change.
  • predictTo predict is to say what you think will happen in the future. Predictions use words such as will, might, may and probably.
  • plan or prediction?Choose the form that fits your message: a plan (going to), an arrangement (present continuous), a timetable (present simple) or a prediction (will / might / may).
  • vary your languageDo not use only will. Use I'm going to, we're meeting, it might, it will probably. A good message uses different forms for different meanings.

Contoh soalan

5 daripada 54 soalan dalam unit ini. Soalan lain, termasuk soalan berajah, soalan bos dan TP6, dimainkan dalam permainan.

  1. TP1

    Which word means 'to copy a file from the internet to your device'?

    1. upload
    2. unplug
    3. uninstall
    4. download
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: D. download
    To download is to copy something from the internet to your own device. The opposite is upload.

  2. TP1

    Which of these is a good way to protect your online privacy?

    1. use a strong password
    2. share your password
    3. click every link
    4. use the same code
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: A. use a strong password
    A strong password keeps your account safe. Never share it.

  3. TP1

    A machine that can do tasks automatically, often shaped like a person, is a

    1. router
    2. robot
    3. printer
    4. screen
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: B. robot
    A robot is a machine that can be programmed to do tasks automatically.

  4. TP2

    I can't come tonight. I ____ my cousins at the airport at 8 o'clock.

    1. am meeting
    2. meet
    3. will have met
    4. have met
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: A. am meeting
    A personal arrangement with a time: present continuous (am meeting).

  5. TP2

    The phone is ringing. I ____ answer it.

    1. have answered
    2. am answering
    3. 'll
    4. answered
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: C. 'll
    A quick decision at the moment of speaking: will ('ll).

Nota ringkas Made in Malaysia

Setiap hentian memberi murid satu kad fakta selepas lulus. Ini kad bagi unit ini:

  • raw materialsRaw materials are the natural materials used to make a product: wood for furniture, cotton for clothes, latex for rubber gloves.
  • export / importTo export is to sell goods to another country. To import is to bring goods in from another country. Malaysia exports palm oil and imports many machines.
  • be + past participlePresent simple: Rubber is collected from trees. Past simple: The factory was built in 1980. Present continuous: A new road is being built. Present perfect: Thousands of cars have been exported.
  • by + agentUse by + agent only when the doer is important or surprising: The song was written by P. Ramlee. Omit it when it is obvious or unknown: My bike was stolen.
  • guided tourA guided tour is a visit led by someone who explains what you see. Many factories offer guided tours for schools.
  • compulsoryCompulsory means you must do it. Optional means you can choose. Safety rules on a factory tour are compulsory.
  • who does it?In passive sentences, the subject receives the action. Listen for was/were + past participle to hear what happened to something: The bag was made in Penang.
  • process wordsListen for first, then, after that, finally. They help you remember the order of stages in a process.
  • heritageHeritage is the traditions, buildings and customs that are passed from one generation to the next. Songket is part of Malaysia's heritage.
  • skillA skill is the ability to do something well, usually after practice. Weaving songket needs a lot of patience and skill.
  • process writingTo describe how something is made: use the present simple passive (is/are + past participle) and sequence words: First, Next, Then, Finally.
  • check the participleIrregular past participles: make → made, write → written, grow → grown, build → built, sell → sold, send → sent, wear → worn.

Contoh soalan

5 daripada 54 soalan dalam unit ini. Soalan lain, termasuk soalan berajah, soalan bos dan TP6, dimainkan dalam permainan.

  1. TP1

    Which word means 'goods that are produced to be sold'?

    1. receipts
    2. salaries
    3. bargains
    4. products
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: D. products
    Products are things that are made or grown to be sold.

  2. TP1

    The money a business has left after paying all its costs is the

    1. profit
    2. packaging
    3. budget
    4. discount
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: A. profit
    Profit is the money earned after all the costs have been paid.

  3. TP1

    A shop has reduced the price of a jacket from RM100 to RM70. This is a

    1. deposit
    2. discount
    3. warranty
    4. service tax
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: B. discount
    A discount is a reduction in price.

  4. TP2

    Rubber ____ from trees in many parts of Malaysia.

    1. collects
    2. is collecting
    3. has collecting
    4. is collected
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: D. is collected
    Present simple passive: is/are + past participle. Rubber is singular, so is collected.

  5. TP2

    The Petronas Towers ____ in 1998.

    1. were completed
    2. are completed
    3. have completed
    4. completed
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: A. were completed
    Past simple passive: was/were + past participle. Towers is plural, so were completed.

TP4: Murid understands spoken English on production, trade and the passive voice and identifies main ideas and specific details well. Murid is working towards exceeding the target.

TP5: Murid reads longer texts on production, trade and the passive voice, makes good inferences and guesses the meaning of unfamiliar words from context. Very good progress.

TP6: Murid uses English accurately and independently on production, trade and the passive voice, corrects errors and builds well-organised sentences. Murid exceeds the target and is an exemplary model to others.

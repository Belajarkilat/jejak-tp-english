Nota ringkas Life on Earth

Setiap hentian memberi murid satu kad fakta selepas lulus. Ini kad bagi unit ini:

  • rhinocerosA rhinoceros (or rhino) has a horn on its nose. Many rhinos are in danger because people hunt them. Say: rye-NOS-uh-rus.
  • reefA coral reef is a line of rock or coral under the sea. Many colourful fish live on a reef.
  • -er / moreShort adjectives: tall → taller → the tallest. Long adjectives: expensive → more expensive → the most expensive. Irregular: good → better → the best, bad → worse → the worst.
  • some / any / a / anUse a/an with one countable noun (a lake, an owl). Use some in positive sentences (some water). Use any in negatives and questions (There aren't any owls. Are there any lakes?).
  • volunteerA volunteer helps and does not get paid (sukarelawan). Example: She is a volunteer at the animal shelter.
  • endangeredAn endangered animal is in danger of disappearing forever. We should protect endangered animals and their homes.
  • habitatA habitat is the natural home of an animal or plant. The habitat of a penguin is cold ice and sea.
  • comparingWhen you listen to a comparison, listen for words like bigger, smaller, the biggest, more dangerous and the most beautiful.
  • protectprotect = keep something safe from harm (melindungi). Example: Rangers protect the forest and the animals that live there.
  • rangersRangers look after national parks. They watch the animals, protect the forest and teach visitors.
  • thanUse than only with a comparative: Penang is smaller than Selangor. Do not use than with a superlative. Use the: Selangor is the biggest.
  • a clear paragraphA good paragraph starts with a topic sentence, then gives details, then ends with a closing sentence.

Contoh soalan

5 daripada 54 soalan dalam unit ini. Soalan lain, termasuk soalan berajah, soalan bos dan TP6, dimainkan dalam permainan.

  1. TP1

    Which word describes a very high, steep rock beside the sea?

    1. cliff
    2. lake
    3. forest
    4. waterfall
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: A. cliff
    A cliff is a high, steep rock face, often next to the sea.

  2. TP1

    Which of these is a large animal that lives in the sea?

    1. butterfly
    2. whale
    3. tiger
    4. gorilla
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: B. whale
    A whale is a very big mammal that lives in the ocean.

  3. TP1

    Which group has ONLY animals?

    1. beach, cliff, lake
    2. bee, island, snake
    3. turtle, whale, tiger
    4. owl, valley, giraffe
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: C. turtle, whale, tiger
    A turtle, a whale and a tiger are animals. Beach, cliff, lake and valley are places.

  4. TP2

    A cheetah is ____ than a horse.

    1. fast
    2. fastest
    3. more fast
    4. faster
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: D. faster
    Comparing two things: fast + -er = faster than.

  5. TP2

    Mount Kinabalu is ____ mountain in Malaysia.

    1. the highest
    2. higher
    3. highest
    4. more high
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: A. the highest
    The highest of all mountains in Malaysia: the + highest.

Contoh ulasan PBD

Selepas murid bermain, Jejak TP English mencadangkan TP dan ulasan seperti di bawah. Guru menyemak, kemudian menyalin ulasan ke dalam rekod PBD.

TP1: Murid recognises a few basic words on animals, landscapes and comparing things with a lot of support. Suggestion: revise the word cards in Stop 1 with a friend and use pictures to remember the meanings.

TP2: Murid understands key vocabulary on animals, landscapes and comparing things but still makes basic grammar errors. Suggestion: practise short sentences using the target grammar every day.

TP3: Murid reads short texts on animals, landscapes and comparing things and finds specific details accurately. Murid achieves the expected target for this unit.

TP4: Murid understands spoken English on animals, landscapes and comparing things and identifies main ideas and specific details well. Murid is working towards exceeding the target.

TP5: Murid reads longer texts on animals, landscapes and comparing things, makes good inferences and guesses the meaning of unfamiliar words from context. Very good progress.

TP6: Murid uses English accurately and independently on animals, landscapes and comparing things, corrects errors and builds well-organised sentences. Murid exceeds the target and is an exemplary model to others.

Nota ringkas He Said, She Said

Setiap hentian memberi murid satu kad fakta selepas lulus. Ini kad bagi unit ini:

  • reporting verbsReporting verbs show what the speaker did: suggest, remind, refuse, agree, deny, accuse, warn, advise, promise, admit, complain.
  • headlineA headline is the title of a news story. It is short and uses strong words so that readers want to read on.
  • backshiftIn reported speech, the verb usually moves one step into the past: 'I am tired.' → She said (that) she was tired. 'I will help.' → He said (that) he would help. Pronouns and time words change: today → that day, tomorrow → the next day.
  • reporting-verb patternsagree to + verb (He agreed to help). refuse to + verb. remind someone to + verb. suggest that + clause (She suggested that we leave early). deny + -ing (He denied taking it). accuse someone of + -ing.
  • interviewAn interview is a formal conversation where one person asks questions and another answers. Journalists interview people to get information.
  • confirmTo confirm is to say that something is definitely true or going to happen. Please confirm your attendance by Friday.
  • who said what?When you listen to a report, notice who said what: Mr Lim said that ... / She told me that ... Reporting verbs show the speaker's attitude.
  • report backAfter you listen, practise reporting what you heard using said that, asked me to, suggested that, refused to.
  • interview reportIn a newspaper interview, journalists often report what the person said: She said that ... He explained that ... She admitted that ... Quotations in inverted commas are exact words.
  • quoteA quote is the exact words that someone said, written inside quotation marks. A reported statement does not use quotation marks and changes the tense.
  • report accuratelyWhen you report, change: tense (am → was), pronouns (I → he), time words (tomorrow → the next day), and use the right reporting verb pattern.
  • common mistakesDo not say 'She told that...' (use 'She told me that...' or 'She said that...'). Do not say 'He suggested me to go' (use 'He suggested that I go').

Contoh soalan

5 daripada 54 soalan dalam unit ini. Soalan lain, termasuk soalan berajah, soalan bos dan TP6, dimainkan dalam permainan.

  1. TP1

    Which word means 'to say that you will not do something'?

    1. agree
    2. suggest
    3. remind
    4. refuse
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: D. refuse
    To refuse is to say firmly that you will not do something.

  2. TP1

    Which verb is used when you say that you did NOT do something wrong?

    1. deny
    2. admit
    3. promise
    4. accept
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: A. deny
    To deny is to say that something is not true.

  3. TP1

    When you give someone an idea of what they could do, you ____ it.

    1. accuse
    2. suggest
    3. refuse
    4. deny
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: B. suggest
    To suggest is to offer an idea or a plan.

  4. TP2

    'I am tired,' said Ben. → Ben said that he ____ tired.

    1. is
    2. will be
    3. has been
    4. was
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: D. was
    Backshift: am → was.

  5. TP2

    'I will call you tomorrow,' she said. → She said that she ____ call me the next day.

    1. would
    2. will
    3. can
    4. has
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: A. would
    Backshift: will → would.

Contoh ulasan PBD

TP1: Murid recognises a few basic words on news, interviews and reported speech with a lot of support. Suggestion: revise the word cards in Stop 1 with a friend and use pictures to remember the meanings.

TP2: Murid understands key vocabulary on news, interviews and reported speech but still makes basic grammar errors. Suggestion: practise short sentences using the target grammar every day.

TP3: Murid reads short texts on news, interviews and reported speech and finds specific details accurately. Murid achieves the expected target for this unit.

TP4: Murid understands spoken English on news, interviews and reported speech and identifies main ideas and specific details well. Murid is working towards exceeding the target.

TP5: Murid reads longer texts on news, interviews and reported speech, makes good inferences and guesses the meaning of unfamiliar words from context. Very good progress.

TP6: Murid uses English accurately and independently on news, interviews and reported speech, corrects errors and builds well-organised sentences. Murid exceeds the target and is an exemplary model to others.

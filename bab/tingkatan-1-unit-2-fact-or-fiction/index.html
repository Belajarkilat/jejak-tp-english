Nota ringkas Fact or fiction?

Setiap hentian memberi murid satu kad fakta selepas lulus. Ini kad bagi unit ini:

  • biographyA biography is a book about a real person's life, written by another person. An autobiography is written by the person about his or her OWN life.
  • explorerAn explorer travels to new places to discover things. Example: Ibn Battuta was a famous explorer.
  • -ed or irregularRegular verbs: add -ed (enjoy → enjoyed). Irregular verbs change: read → read, see → saw, go → went, have → had. In negatives and questions, use did + the BASE verb: I didn't see. Did you see?
  • was / wereI / he / she / it was. You / we / they were. Negative: wasn't / weren't. Question: Was he...? Were they...?
  • exhibitionAn exhibition is a public show of art or objects, for example in a museum. Example: The exhibition is open every day except Monday.
  • admissionAdmission = the price to enter a place (harga masuk). Free admission means you do not need to pay.
  • yesterdayTime words for the past: yesterday, last night, last week, two days ago, in 2010. Listen for them to know the story is in the past.
  • sequenceSequencing words: first, then, after that, finally. They show the ORDER of events in a story.
  • inspireinspire = make someone want to do something good (memberi inspirasi). Example: Her story inspired many young girls.
  • never gave upNever give up = keep trying even when it is difficult. A biography often shows how a person never gave up.
  • sequencingUse first, then, after that and finally to put your story in order. Put a comma after them: First, we went to the park.
  • past simple storyA past story uses the past simple for the main events: We arrived at nine o'clock. Remember that irregular verbs change: went, saw, had, ate.

Contoh soalan

3 daripada 54 soalan dalam unit ini. Soalan lain, termasuk soalan berajah, soalan bos dan TP6, dimainkan dalam permainan.

  1. TP2

    Yesterday, I ____ the book in class.

    1. reads
    2. am reading
    3. readed
    4. read
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: D. read
    Yesterday shows the past, so we use the past simple. The past of read is read (spelled the same, pronounced like red).

  2. TP2

    She ____ the film adaptation of the novel last year.

    1. didn't saw
    2. didn't see
    3. don't see
    4. doesn't see
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: B. didn't see
    The negative of the past simple is didn't + BASE verb: didn't see (not didn't saw).

  3. TP2

    ____ you finish the book last night?

    1. Do
    2. Were
    3. Did
    4. Are
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: C. Did
    Questions in the past simple start with Did + subject + base verb: Did you finish...?

Contoh ulasan PBD

Selepas murid bermain, Jejak TP English mencadangkan TP dan ulasan seperti di bawah. Guru menyemak, kemudian menyalin ulasan ke dalam rekod PBD.

TP1: Murid recognises a few basic words on books, professions and the past simple with a lot of support. Suggestion: revise the word cards in Stop 1 with a friend and use pictures to remember the meanings.

TP2: Murid understands key vocabulary on books, professions and the past simple but still makes basic grammar errors. Suggestion: practise short sentences using the target grammar every day.

TP3: Murid reads short texts on books, professions and the past simple and finds specific details accurately. Murid achieves the expected target for this unit.

TP4: Murid understands spoken English on books, professions and the past simple and identifies main ideas and specific details well. Murid is working towards exceeding the target.

TP5: Murid reads longer texts on books, professions and the past simple, makes good inferences and guesses the meaning of unfamiliar words from context. Very good progress.

TP6: Murid uses English accurately and independently on books, professions and the past simple, corrects errors and builds well-organised sentences. Murid exceeds the target and is an exemplary model to others.

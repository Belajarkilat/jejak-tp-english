Nota ringkas Festivals and Friends

Setiap hentian memberi murid satu kad fakta selepas lulus. Ini kad bagi unit ini:

  • word formationAdd endings to a root word to change its class: celebrate → celebration (noun), friend → friendship (noun), care → careful (adjective), happy → happily (adverb).
  • traditionA tradition is a custom that has been passed down for a long time. Open house, red packets and lighting oil lamps are Malaysian traditions.
  • adverbs of manner and comparisonAdverbs describe verbs: She sings beautifully. To compare: She sings more beautifully than me. He works the hardest of all. Irregular: well → better → the best.
  • articles and pronounsa/an for one of many, the for something known, no article for general plurals (Children love festivals). someone/everyone/no one + singular verb: Everyone is happy.
  • traditionalTraditional means following customs that have existed for a long time: traditional dances, traditional food, traditional costumes.
  • admission freeAdmission free means you do not pay to enter. A donation is optional money that people give if they want to help.
  • how it was saidAdverbs tell you how something was done: slowly, carefully, loudly, enthusiastically. They often tell you the speaker's attitude, too.
  • everyone / no oneIndefinite pronouns show how many people: everyone (all), someone (one person), no one (nobody). Listen for them to understand the number of people involved.
  • hospitalityHospitality means being friendly and generous to guests. Malaysian open houses are famous for their hospitality.
  • diverseDiverse means made up of many different types. A diverse country has people of many cultures, languages and religions.
  • vivid languageUse adverbs of manner to make your description vivid: The dancers moved gracefully. The crowd cheered loudly. Add an article before a singular noun: a lantern, an open house, the stage.
  • proofreadProofread for: adverb or adjective? article needed? singular verb after everyone / someone / no one?

Contoh soalan

5 daripada 54 soalan dalam unit ini. Soalan lain, termasuk soalan berajah, soalan bos dan TP6, dimainkan dalam permainan.

  1. TP1

    What is the noun for the act of celebrating?

    1. celebrities
    2. celebratory
    3. celebrated
    4. celebration
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: D. celebration
    Celebration is the noun for the act of celebrating.

  2. TP1

    What is the noun from the verb 'friend' (the state of being friends)?

    1. friendship
    2. friendly
    3. befriend
    4. friendless
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: A. friendship
    Add -ship to make the noun: friendship.

  3. TP1

    Complete the sentence with the adjective from 'care': Please be ____ with the lanterns. They can easily catch fire.

    1. carelessly
    2. careful
    3. caring
    4. carer
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: B. careful
    Careful (adjective) means taking care to avoid mistakes or danger.

  4. TP2

    The dancers moved ____ than the musicians, so the audience clapped louder.

    1. most graceful ones
    2. more graceful ones
    3. most gracefully
    4. more gracefully
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: D. more gracefully
    Adverb of manner, comparative: more gracefully than.

  5. TP2

    Of all the singers, Hana sang ____.

    1. the most beautifully
    2. more beautifully
    3. the most beautiful
    4. the beautifullest
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: A. the most beautifully
    Superlative adverb: the most beautifully (of all).

Contoh ulasan PBD

Selepas murid bermain, Jejak TP English mencadangkan TP dan ulasan seperti di bawah. Guru menyemak, kemudian menyalin ulasan ke dalam rekod PBD.

TP1: Murid recognises a few basic words on festivals, adverbs, articles and indefinite pronouns with a lot of support. Suggestion: revise the word cards in Stop 1 with a friend and use pictures to remember the meanings.

TP2: Murid understands key vocabulary on festivals, adverbs, articles and indefinite pronouns but still makes basic grammar errors. Suggestion: practise short sentences using the target grammar every day.

TP3: Murid reads short texts on festivals, adverbs, articles and indefinite pronouns and finds specific details accurately. Murid achieves the expected target for this unit.

TP4: Murid understands spoken English on festivals, adverbs, articles and indefinite pronouns and identifies main ideas and specific details well. Murid is working towards exceeding the target.

TP5: Murid reads longer texts on festivals, adverbs, articles and indefinite pronouns, makes good inferences and guesses the meaning of unfamiliar words from context. Very good progress.

TP6: Murid uses English accurately and independently on festivals, adverbs, articles and indefinite pronouns, corrects errors and builds well-organised sentences. Murid exceeds the target and is an exemplary model to others.

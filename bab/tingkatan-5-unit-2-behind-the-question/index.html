Nota ringkas Behind the Question

Setiap hentian memberi murid satu kad fakta selepas lulus. Ini kad bagi unit ini:

  • interview vocabularyapplicant = a person who applies for a job. qualification = an exam or course that you have passed. experience = knowledge from doing something. reference = a person who recommends you.
  • questions an interviewer asksTypical questions: Why do you want this job? What are your strengths? Have you worked in a team before? Where do you see yourself in five years?
  • backshift chartpresent simple → past simple; present continuous → past continuous; past simple → past perfect; present perfect → past perfect; will → would; can → could. Time words: today → that day, now → then, tomorrow → the next day, yesterday → the day before, here → there.
  • reported questionsReported questions use statement word order, with no question mark: 'Where do you live?' → She asked where I lived. 'Do you like football?' → He asked if (whether) I liked football.
  • vacancyA vacancy is a job that is available. Companies advertise vacancies and ask for applications. Closing date = the last day to apply.
  • requirementsRequirements are the things that an applicant must have: qualifications, skills or experience. A good application matches the requirements.
  • hear the reportingWhen you hear said that / asked if / wanted to know whether, someone is reporting. Listen for the changed tenses and for words such as the day before and the next day.
  • who asked what?In a long conversation, identify the speaker (interviewer or applicant) and the question that was asked. Then check what the applicant answered.
  • strength and weaknessIn interviews, you are often asked about your strengths (what you do well) and weaknesses (what you want to improve). Be honest and give examples.
  • confidentA confident person believes in their own abilities. Confidence is shown by calm voice, eye contact and clear answers.
  • reporting an interviewIn a report: use said that / told me that / asked whether; change the tense; change the time and place words; do not use question marks for reported questions.
  • check theseCheck: tense moved back? pronouns changed? word order of reported questions? time words changed? punctuation correct?

Contoh soalan

5 daripada 54 soalan dalam unit ini. Soalan lain, termasuk soalan berajah, soalan bos dan TP6, dimainkan dalam permainan.

  1. TP1

    A person who is responsible for hiring workers is the

    1. passenger
    2. tourist
    3. patient
    4. employer
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: D. employer
    An employer hires and pays workers.

  2. TP1

    Your teacher wrote a letter saying that you are hardworking. This letter is a

    1. reference
    2. receipt
    3. ticket
    4. warning
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: A. reference
    A reference is a statement that recommends a person.

  3. TP1

    Which word means 'the money you earn for a job every month'?

    1. discount
    2. salary
    3. donation
    4. tax
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: B. salary
    A salary is money paid regularly for a job.

  4. TP2

    'I work in a bank,' she said. → She said that she ____ in a bank.

    1. works
    2. has worked
    3. will work
    4. worked
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: D. worked
    Present simple → past simple: worked.

  5. TP2

    'I bought a new phone,' he said. → He said that he ____ a new phone.

    1. had bought
    2. bought
    3. has bought
    4. buys
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: A. had bought
    Past simple → past perfect: had bought.

Contoh ulasan PBD

Selepas murid bermain, Jejak TP English mencadangkan TP dan ulasan seperti di bawah. Guru menyemak, kemudian menyalin ulasan ke dalam rekod PBD.

TP1: Murid recognises a few basic words on interviews, careers and reported speech with a lot of support. Suggestion: revise the word cards in Stop 1 with a friend and use pictures to remember the meanings.

TP2: Murid understands key vocabulary on interviews, careers and reported speech but still makes basic grammar errors. Suggestion: practise short sentences using the target grammar every day.

TP3: Murid reads short texts on interviews, careers and reported speech and finds specific details accurately. Murid achieves the expected target for this unit.

TP4: Murid understands spoken English on interviews, careers and reported speech and identifies main ideas and specific details well. Murid is working towards exceeding the target.

TP5: Murid reads longer texts on interviews, careers and reported speech, makes good inferences and guesses the meaning of unfamiliar words from context. Very good progress.

TP6: Murid uses English accurately and independently on interviews, careers and reported speech, corrects errors and builds well-organised sentences. Murid exceeds the target and is an exemplary model to others.

Jimat masa merekod PBD. Murid bermain di telefon, dan jadual TP kelas siap dengan sendirinya.

Nota ringkas Healthy Opinions

Setiap hentian memberi murid satu kad fakta selepas lulus. Ini kad bagi unit ini:

  • in my opinionPhrases for opinions: In my opinion, ... / I believe that ... / As far as I'm concerned, ... / It seems to me that ... Give a reason with because.
  • it makes me feelDescribe mood: Exercise makes me feel energetic. Too much homework makes me feel stressed. Use feel + an adjective: relaxed, nervous, confident.
  • So do I / Neither do ITo agree with a positive sentence: I love swimming. → So do I. To agree with a negative sentence: I don't like fast food. → Neither do I. Use the same auxiliary verb: I can swim. → So can I.
  • allowed tocan / be allowed to = permission. can't / not be allowed to = prohibition. could / was allowed to = permission in the past. was able to = managed to do something in the past.
  • membershipMembership is the state of belonging to a club or gym. Members usually have a card and some special rights.
  • guestA guest is a person who visits. Some gyms allow members to bring a guest, but the guest may have to pay a small fee.
  • agreementListen to how people agree and disagree: So do I! / Neither do I. / I'm not so sure. / That's true, but ... They show the speakers' attitude.
  • connecting wordsIn addition, however, as a result, to summarise: Connecting words help you follow the speaker's argument and conclusion.
  • persuadeTo persuade is to make someone agree with your opinion or do something. A good writer persuades with reasons and examples.
  • balanced viewA balanced view looks at both sides of an issue. Words such as however, on the other hand and although introduce the other side.
  • opinion paragraphOpinion paragraph: 1. state your opinion (In my opinion, ...). 2. give a reason (because ...). 3. add another point (In addition, ...). 4. conclude (To summarise, ...).
  • agree politelyPolite agreement and disagreement: I agree, but ... / That's true. However, ... / I see your point, but I think ...

Contoh soalan

5 daripada 54 soalan dalam unit ini. Soalan lain, termasuk soalan berajah, soalan bos dan TP6, dimainkan dalam permainan.

  1. TP1

    Which phrase is used to give an opinion?

    1. As a result
    2. For example
    3. In addition
    4. In my opinion
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: D. In my opinion
    In my opinion shows that you are giving your own view.

  2. TP1

    Which word means 'a bad habit that is hard to stop'?

    1. addiction
    2. exercise
    3. vitamin
    4. routine
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: A. addiction
    An addiction is a strong need to do something, such as smoking or gaming.

  3. TP1

    A diet that has all the nutrients that your body needs is

    1. limited
    2. balanced
    3. unusual
    4. expensive
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: B. balanced
    A balanced diet includes the right amounts of different kinds of food.

  4. TP2

    'I really enjoy swimming.' — '____.'

    1. Neither do I
    2. So I do
    3. I don't too
    4. So do I
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: D. So do I
    To agree with a positive sentence with enjoy (simple present), we say So do I.

  5. TP2

    'I don't like fast food.' — '____.'

    1. Neither do I
    2. So do I too
    3. Neither I do
    4. I do either then
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: A. Neither do I
    To agree with a negative sentence, we say Neither do I.

TP3: Murid reads short texts on health, opinions, agreement and permission and finds specific details accurately. Murid achieves the expected target for this unit.

TP4: Murid understands spoken English on health, opinions, agreement and permission and identifies main ideas and specific details well. Murid is working towards exceeding the target.

TP5: Murid reads longer texts on health, opinions, agreement and permission, makes good inferences and guesses the meaning of unfamiliar words from context. Very good progress.

TP6: Murid uses English accurately and independently on health, opinions, agreement and permission, corrects errors and builds well-organised sentences. Murid exceeds the target and is an exemplary model to others.

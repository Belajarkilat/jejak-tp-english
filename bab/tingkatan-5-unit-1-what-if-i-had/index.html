Nota ringkas What If I Had...?

Setiap hentian memberi murid satu kad fakta selepas lulus. Ini kad bagi unit ini:

  • regretA regret is a feeling of sadness about something that you did or did not do. We often say: I regret not studying harder. I wish I had listened to my teacher.
  • consequenceA consequence is a result of an action. Good decisions have positive consequences, and careless decisions can have serious consequences.
  • third conditionalIf + past perfect, would have + past participle. It talks about an unreal situation in the past: If I had studied harder, I would have passed. (but I didn't study and I didn't pass).
  • wish + past perfectI wish / If only + past perfect expresses regret about the past: I wish I had listened to my parents. If only we had left earlier, we wouldn't have missed the train.
  • missedTo miss something is to fail to catch, see or attend it. If you miss a bus, you fail to catch it. You can also miss a person, which means feeling sad because they are not with you.
  • apologiseTo apologise is to say that you are sorry. We apologise for something: I apologise for being late.
  • hear the regretWhen you hear I wish + had / If only + had / if + had ... would have, the speaker is talking about the past and feels regret. The real situation is the opposite of what the speaker says.
  • opposite meaningIf I had studied, I would have passed. = I didn't study, so I didn't pass. Listen carefully to the real situation behind the sentence.
  • reflectTo reflect is to think carefully about something that happened, so that you can learn from it. A reflective writer asks: What did I do? What would I do differently?
  • turning pointA turning point is a moment when something important changes. A bad experience can become a turning point if you learn from it.
  • reflective writingReflective paragraph: 1. what happened (past simple), 2. what you regret (I wish I had ...), 3. what you would have done (If I had ..., I would have ...), 4. what you learnt (present perfect/simple).
  • check the auxiliariesThird conditional: had + past participle in the if clause; would have + past participle in the result. Never use would in the if clause.

Contoh soalan

5 daripada 54 soalan dalam unit ini. Soalan lain, termasuk soalan berajah, soalan bos dan TP6, dimainkan dalam permainan.

  1. TP1

    Which word means 'to take a chance, even though the result could be bad'?

    1. refuse
    2. rescue
    3. remind
    4. risk
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: D. risk
    To take a risk is to do something that might have a bad result.

  2. TP1

    When you think carefully about the good and bad points before you decide, you ____ your options.

    1. consider
    2. ignore
    3. delay
    4. forget
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: A. consider
    To consider is to think carefully about something.

  3. TP1

    Which word means 'a chance to do something good'?

    1. argument
    2. crisis
    3. opportunity
    4. limitation
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: C. opportunity
    An opportunity is a chance to do something good.

  4. TP2

    If I ____ the alarm, I would have caught the bus.

    1. heard
    2. would hear
    3. have heard
    4. had heard
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: D. had heard
    Third conditional: if + past perfect (had heard).

  5. TP2

    If she had studied, she ____ the exam.

    1. would have passed
    2. will certainly pass
    3. would surely pass
    4. had already passed
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: A. would have passed
    Third conditional result clause: would have + past participle.

Contoh ulasan PBD

Selepas murid bermain, Jejak TP English mencadangkan TP dan ulasan seperti di bawah. Guru menyemak, kemudian menyalin ulasan ke dalam rekod PBD.

TP1: Murid recognises a few basic words on decisions, regrets and the third conditional with a lot of support. Suggestion: revise the word cards in Stop 1 with a friend and use pictures to remember the meanings.

TP2: Murid understands key vocabulary on decisions, regrets and the third conditional but still makes basic grammar errors. Suggestion: practise short sentences using the target grammar every day.

TP3: Murid reads short texts on decisions, regrets and the third conditional and finds specific details accurately. Murid achieves the expected target for this unit.

TP4: Murid understands spoken English on decisions, regrets and the third conditional and identifies main ideas and specific details well. Murid is working towards exceeding the target.

TP5: Murid reads longer texts on decisions, regrets and the third conditional, makes good inferences and guesses the meaning of unfamiliar words from context. Very good progress.

TP6: Murid uses English accurately and independently on decisions, regrets and the third conditional, corrects errors and builds well-organised sentences. Murid exceeds the target and is an exemplary model to others.

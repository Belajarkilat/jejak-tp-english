Nota ringkas Discover Malaysia

Setiap hentian memberi murid satu kad fakta selepas lulus. Ini kad bagi unit ini:

  • heritage siteA heritage site is a place that is protected because of its historical or cultural value. George Town and Melaka are UNESCO World Heritage Sites.
  • attractionA tourist attraction is a place that people visit for fun or interest, such as a beach, a museum or a waterfall.
  • defining and non-definingDefining (no commas, needed): The girl who lives next door is a guide. Non-defining (commas, extra information): Penang, which is famous for its food, is an island. In non-defining clauses, do not use that.
  • participle clauses-ing clause (active): The man standing at the gate is our guide. (= who is standing). -ed clause (passive): The temple built in 1800 is very famous. (= which was built). Whose shows possession: the man whose bag was stolen.
  • packageA holiday package is a trip that includes transport, accommodation and sometimes meals. It is often cheaper than booking each part separately.
  • included / excludedIncluded means that it is part of the price. Excluded means that it is not part of the price and you must pay extra.
  • extra informationListen for extra information in the middle of a sentence: Penang, which is famous for its food, ... The extra information is spoken with a pause, like commas in writing.
  • who, which, where, whoseThe relative pronoun tells you what the clause describes: who → a person, which → a thing, where → a place, whose → something that belongs to a person.
  • atmosphereThe atmosphere of a place is the feeling that it has: peaceful, lively, romantic. Travel writers describe the atmosphere so that readers can imagine it.
  • recommendTo recommend is to say that something is good and that others should try it. Travel blogs often recommend places, food and activities.
  • combine sentencesJoin two sentences with a relative pronoun: Langkawi is an island. It is famous for its beaches. → Langkawi, which is famous for its beaches, is an island. Use commas for extra information only.
  • vary your sentencesGood descriptions mix short and long sentences. Use relative clauses and participle clauses to add detail: a village surrounded by mountains; a market selling local fruit.

Contoh soalan

5 daripada 54 soalan dalam unit ini. Soalan lain, termasuk soalan berajah, soalan bos dan TP6, dimainkan dalam permainan.

  1. TP1

    Which word means 'typical of a place and showing its special features'?

    1. artificial
    2. ordinary
    3. modern
    4. authentic
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: D. authentic
    Authentic means real and genuine.

  2. TP1

    A beautiful place that is not yet spoiled by tourists is

    1. unspoilt
    2. crowded
    3. polluted
    4. noisy
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: A. unspoilt
    Unspoilt means in its natural, original condition.

  3. TP1

    A holiday plan that lists the places to visit and times is an

    1. invoice
    2. itinerary
    3. inventory
    4. invitation
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: B. itinerary
    An itinerary is a detailed plan of a journey.

  4. TP2

    The man ____ showed us around the museum was very friendly.

    1. which
    2. whose
    3. where
    4. who
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: D. who
    Who is used for people in a defining relative clause.

  5. TP2

    This is the village ____ my grandparents were born.

    1. where
    2. who
    3. which
    4. whose
    Lihat jawapan

    Jawapan: A. where
    Where refers to a place.
